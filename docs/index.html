How it works

From Samsung Notes to your ZIP.

SNote Retrieval keeps the process simple. The application automatically looks for the required Samsung Notes data and guides you if your notes need to be synced first.

01

Download SNote Retrieval

Download the Windows application and open it. SNote Retrieval automatically looks for the files required to read your Samsung Notes.

If Samsung Notes isn't ready, the application guides you through installing or opening Samsung Notes and syncing your notes.

02

Press “Extract Now”

Once your Samsung Notes data is ready, press Extract Now.

The extraction happens locally on your computer. You don't need an internet connection to extract your notes.

03

Browse your folders and notes

SNote Retrieval shows you a preview of your extracted data before you purchase a license.

Browse your existing folders and nested folders in the Folders tab, or browse all notes in the Notes tab.

Click any note to see its file name and content.

File extensions

Choose the extension you want.

Notes are exported as .md files by default. If you want another extension, change the extension on a note in the preview.

The extension you choose is automatically applied to all exported notes.

Changing the extension does not change the contents of your notes. It only changes the file name and extension.

When you're ready

Purchase only after you've checked your preview.

When you click Save for the first time, SNote Retrieval takes you to the license page.

Click Get A License to open the payment website in your default browser. After your one-time purchase, you'll receive your license key on the purchase page and by email.

Copy the key, paste it into SNote Retrieval, and activate your license.

Export

Choose where your ZIP belongs.

Once your license is activated, return to the preview and click Save. Choose the destination folder and SNote Retrieval creates your ZIP file there.

After saving, SNote Retrieval automatically opens the folder containing your exported ZIP.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the preview free?

Yes. You can download SNote Retrieval and check your folders and notes in the preview before purchasing a license.

Does SNote Retrieval preserve my folders?

Yes. The folders and nested folders you created in Samsung Notes are preserved in the exported ZIP.

Can I select which notes to export?

No. SNote Retrieval exports the notes shown in the preview.

Can I see my note contents before purchasing?

Yes. Click any note in the Folders or Notes tab to see its file name and content.

What is the default file format?

Notes are exported as .md files by default.

Can I change .md to .txt?

Yes. You can change the extension in the preview. The chosen extension is applied automatically to all exported notes. Changing the extension does not change the note content.

Does extraction require internet?

No. The extraction process works offline. Internet access is required for license verification.

Is SNote Retrieval affiliated with Samsung?

No. SNote Retrieval is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Samsung Electronics or Samsung Notes.

Is the license a subscription?

No. SNote Retrieval uses a one-time purchase.

How do I receive my license key?

After purchasing, your license key is provided to you and is also sent to your email address.
