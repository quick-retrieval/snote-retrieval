Quick Retrieval ("Quick Retrieval", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with SNote Retrieval, our website, application, and related services.

SNote Retrieval is a Windows application designed to assist users in extracting and exporting data from Samsung Notes. The application reads the relevant Samsung Notes data locally on the user's computer in order to provide its export functionality.

We take privacy seriously and have designed SNote Retrieval to minimize the information transmitted outside the user's computer.

SNote Retrieval is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or officially connected with Samsung Electronics or Samsung Notes. For more information, please see our Third-Party & Trademark Disclaimer .

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We Collect Directly

Quick Retrieval does not operate user accounts for SNote Retrieval and does not directly collect or maintain users' Samsung Notes data.

We may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as information contained in an email that you choose to send to our support address.

Purchase and license-related information is handled through our payment and licensing provider, Dodo Payments, as described in this Privacy Policy.

1.2 Payment and License Information

Purchases and license management for SNote Retrieval are handled through Dodo Payments.

Dodo Payments may request information necessary to process your purchase and provide its services, including your email address. The email address associated with your purchase may also be used to access Dodo Payments' customer portal and manage your license.

Quick Retrieval does not store your payment card details, bank details, or complete payment credentials.

Information collected and processed directly by Dodo Payments is subject to Dodo Payments' own privacy policy and terms.

2. Samsung Notes Data

SNote Retrieval needs to read Samsung Notes data locally on your Windows computer in order to provide its export functionality.

The application processes the relevant data on your computer. This local processing is necessary for the application to identify and export the information you have selected.

Samsung Notes data processed by SNote Retrieval is not uploaded to Quick Retrieval or stored on our servers.

Quick Retrieval does not have access to the Samsung Notes data that is read and processed locally by the application.

The application does not transmit your Samsung Notes data to us for storage, analysis, or processing.

You remain responsible for the data stored on your computer and for maintaining appropriate backups of important information.

3. License Verification

An internet connection is required for SNote Retrieval to verify and manage your software license.

License verification is handled through Dodo Payments.

To allow you to identify which computer a license is associated with, SNote Retrieval may provide Dodo Payments with the device name associated with the installation.

The device information is provided in the following general format:

Windows PC (<device name>)

This allows you to recognize the device associated with your license when managing your license through the applicable license-management functionality.

The license-verification connection is not used to upload your Samsung Notes data.

4. Information We Do Not Automatically Collect

SNote Retrieval is designed without telemetry or automatic collection of information about your use of the application.

SNote Retrieval does not automatically send the following to Quick Retrieval:

  • Samsung Notes data;
  • Error logs;
  • Crash reports;
  • Usage statistics; or
  • Analytics information.

We also do not intentionally collect your IP address or use SNote Retrieval to collect unrelated personal information from your computer.

The application's internet connection is used for license verification and related license functionality.

5. Error Reporting and Support

SNote Retrieval does not automatically send error logs, crash reports, or diagnostic information to Quick Retrieval.

In certain situations, the application may provide an option to open an email containing pre-filled information that may help us understand an error.

The email is opened for you to review before it is sent. You may edit, remove, or add information before sending it.

The application does not automatically send the email.

You decide whether to send the email and what information to include.

If you voluntarily send an email to our support address, we may receive and process the information contained in that email for the purpose of responding to you or resolving the issue you reported.

We recommend removing any personal, confidential, or sensitive information that is not necessary for support before sending an email.

Support emails may be deleted after the relevant issue has been resolved or when they are no longer reasonably necessary.
