How to Transfer Samsung Notes to iPhone With Folders

Switching from Samsung to iPhone does not mean you have to rebuild your Samsung Notes folders manually. This guide shows how to export your notes with their folder structure, move the exported files to your iPhone, and open them in Obsidian.

SNote Retrieval guide • Windows + iPhone • Markdown + Obsidian
Short answer

Export your Samsung Notes with folders using SNote Retrieval, save the export as a ZIP, transfer that ZIP to your iPhone, extract it with the Files app, and copy the resulting folders into an Obsidian vault.

Before you start

Why transferring Samsung Notes can be difficult

Samsung Notes can contain years of personal notes, work notes, lists, ideas, and other information. If those notes are organized into folders and nested folders, moving the content to another phone can be more complicated than simply copying individual files.

The goal of this workflow is to turn your notes into regular Markdown files while keeping the folder organization represented in the export.

The result is a collection of files that can be transferred to your iPhone and placed inside an Obsidian vault.

Step 1

Export your Samsung Notes with folders

The first part of the process happens on your Windows computer. SNote Retrieval is used to read your Samsung Notes and prepare the export.

01

Download SNote Retrieval

Open the SNote Retrieval website on your Windows computer and download the application.

Get SNote Retrieval
02

Open your Samsung Notes export

Open SNote Retrieval and follow the application's instructions to access your Samsung Notes data.

The application lets you preview your notes and the folders detected in the export before you save the final files.

03

Check your folders and notes

Before saving the export, browse through your folders and notes. This gives you an opportunity to confirm that the content you expect is available.

Free preview

SNote Retrieval provides a free preview so you can inspect your folders and notes before purchasing a license and saving the export.

04

Save the export as a ZIP

When you save the export, your notes are exported as Markdown files inside a ZIP archive. The folder structure is represented by the folders inside that archive.

For example, an export can look like this:

Example export structure 
notes.zip
├── Personal/
│   ├── Ideas.md
│   └── Journal.md
├── Work/
│   ├── Meetings/
│   │   ├── Meeting 1.md
│   │   └── Meeting 2.md
│   └── Projects.md
└── Shopping/
└── Shopping List.md
The important part

You don't need to create folders manually. SNote Retrieval keeps your existing folders and subfolders in the ZIP file.

Step 2

Transfer the ZIP file to your iPhone

After the export is finished, you should have a ZIP file on your Windows computer. The next step is simply to get that file onto your iPhone.

01

Choose a way to transfer the file

You can use a file-transfer method that works for you. For example, you can upload the ZIP to a cloud storage service and download it on your iPhone.

The important thing is that the notes.zip file becomes available in the Files app on your iPhone.

02

Find the ZIP in the Files app

On your iPhone, open the Files app and navigate to the location where you saved or downloaded the ZIP file.

Step 3

Extract the ZIP on your iPhone

You do not need to put the ZIP file directly into Obsidian. First, extract it so that you have the actual folders and Markdown files.

01

Tap the ZIP file

In the Files app, locate notes.zip and tap it.

The Files app can extract the archive and create a folder containing the exported files.

02

Open the extracted folder

Open the extracted folder and check that your folders and Markdown files are there.

Do not skip this step

The ZIP is only the container used to move everything together. Obsidian needs the extracted Markdown files and folders, not the ZIP archive itself.

Step 4

Create an Obsidian vault on your iPhone

Obsidian can work with Markdown files stored in folders. That makes it a convenient destination for the exported notes.

01

Install Obsidian

Install and open the Obsidian app on your iPhone.

Install Obsidian
02

Create a new vault

Create a new local vault in Obsidian. You can choose any name you like.

My Samsung Notes

The vault name is only an example. Use whatever name makes sense to you.

Step 5

Copy the exported folders into your vault

Now that the ZIP has been extracted and your Obsidian vault exists, copy the exported folders and Markdown files into the vault.

01

Open the Files app

Open Files on your iPhone and locate the local Obsidian vault.

Depending on your setup, a local vault may be accessible through a path similar to:

On My iPhone → Obsidian → YOUR_VAULT_NAME
02

Open the extracted notes

Go back to the folder where you extracted notes.zip.

You should see the folders and Markdown files that came from your Samsung Notes export.

03

Copy the folders into the Obsidian vault

Select the exported folders and copy or move them into your Obsidian vault.

On My iPhone → Obsidian → YOUR_VAULT_NAME
Keep the folders intact

Copy the folders themselves rather than moving every Markdown file into one large folder. This is what keeps the organization of your exported notes.

04

Open the vault in Obsidian

Return to Obsidian and open your vault.

Your imported folders and Markdown notes should now be visible in the Obsidian file explorer.

Final check

Check that your notes were imported correctly

Before considering the migration finished, open a few notes from different folders and make sure everything looks as expected.

  • ✓ Your main folders are visible.
  • ✓ Your nested folders are in the expected locations.
  • ✓ Markdown notes open correctly in Obsidian.
  • ✓ Important notes from different folders are present.
What you should end up with

Your folder structure stays organized

The purpose of this workflow is to move the notes as a collection of files and folders rather than manually recreating your organization one note at a time.

Example Obsidian vault

│   ├── Ideas.md
│   └── Journal.md
├── Work/
│   ├── Meetings/
│   │   ├── Monday.md
│   │   └── Friday.md
│   └── Projects.md
└── Shopping/
└── Shopping List.md

In other words, the basic workflow is:

Samsung Notes → SNote Retrieval → ZIP → iPhone Files → Obsidian

Important to know

Exported notes may not look exactly the same

Samsung Notes has its own editing features and formatting. When notes are converted into Markdown files, the result is intended to be usable as regular files outside Samsung Notes.

Some Samsung Notes-specific features may not transfer identically

Formatting or content that depends on features specific to Samsung Notes may not behave exactly the same way after export as Markdown and opening the files in another application.

For this reason, it is a good idea to use the SNote Retrieval preview to inspect your notes and folders before purchasing and saving the export.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I transfer Samsung Notes to iPhone with folders?

Yes. SNote Retrieval can export Samsung Notes while preserving the folders and nested folders represented in the export. The resulting ZIP can then be transferred to your iPhone and the exported Markdown files can be copied into an Obsidian vault.

Does SNote Retrieval preserve Samsung Notes folders?

Preserving the folder structure is a core part of the SNote Retrieval export workflow. Existing folders and nested folders are included in the exported structure.

Can I preview my Samsung Notes before purchasing?

Yes. SNote Retrieval provides a free preview where you can browse your folders and notes before purchasing a license and saving the export.

What file format does SNote Retrieval export?

SNote Retrieval exports notes as .md Markdown files by default. The Markdown files are placed inside the preserved folder structure.

Can I transfer Samsung Notes directly into Obsidian?

The workflow uses SNote Retrieval to export Samsung Notes first. The exported ZIP is then transferred to your iPhone, extracted, and the resulting folders and Markdown files are copied into your Obsidian vault.

Do I copy the ZIP file into Obsidian?

No. Extract the ZIP first. Then copy the extracted folders and Markdown files into your Obsidian vault.

Where is my Obsidian vault on iPhone?

If your vault is stored locally, its files can be accessible through the Files app. Depending on your setup, you may see a location similar to:

On My iPhone → Obsidian → YOUR_VAULT_NAME
