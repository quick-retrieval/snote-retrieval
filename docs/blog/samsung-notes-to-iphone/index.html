Samsung Notes migration

How to Transfer Samsung Notes to iPhone With Folders

Switching from Samsung to iPhone does not mean you have to rebuild your Samsung Notes folders manually. This guide shows how to export your notes with their folder structure, move the exported files to your iPhone, and open them in Obsidian.

SNote Retrieval guide • iPhone + Files • Obsidian
Short answer

First export your Samsung Notes with SNote Retrieval. If you do not have the ZIP yet, download SNote Retrieval below. Then move the ZIP to your iPhone, extract it, and copy the extracted folders into your local Obsidian vault through the Files app.

    Before you start

    What you should have ready

    If you already have a SNote Retrieval ZIP, you can continue with this guide. If you do not have one yet, download SNote Retrieval first and use it to export your Samsung Notes.

    Don't have the ZIP yet?

    Download SNote Retrieval, export your Samsung Notes, and create the ZIP before continuing with the iPhone steps.

    Step 1

    Create an Obsidian vault

    Start by opening Obsidian on your iPhone and creating the local vault that will contain your imported Samsung Notes.

    Install Obsidian

    Install and open the Obsidian app on your iPhone.

    Install Obsidian
    01

    Create a new vault

    Open Obsidian and choose the option to create a new vault. Make sure you create a local vault on the iPhone.

    02

    Name your vault

    Give your vault a name. You can use any name you like; the example below uses a simple name for the imported notes.

    Step 2

    Find the SNote Retrieval ZIP

    The SNote Retrieval export is saved as a ZIP file. Transfer that ZIP to your iPhone using the method that works for you, then open the Files app.

    03

    Locate the ZIP

    Find the exported ZIP in the Files app. The example export is named Notes.zip.

    The ZIP is only the container

    You do not need to open the ZIP inside Obsidian. First extract it so the folders and Markdown files become available as normal files.

    Step 3

    Extract the ZIP on your iPhone

    Use the Files app to extract the ZIP. Once extraction is complete, you should have the exported folders available separately from the ZIP file.

    04

    Open the extracted folders

    After extraction, open the resulting folder and check that your note folders and files are present.

    Do not copy the ZIP itself

    Work with the extracted folders. Those folders contain the actual exported notes that you want to place inside the Obsidian vault.

    Step 4

    Copy the extracted folders

    Select the folders created from the SNote Retrieval export and copy them. Keep the folder hierarchy intact so your notes remain organized.

    05

    Select and copy the folders

    In Files, select the exported folders and choose Copy.

    Keep the folders together

    The folders are what preserve the organization of your exported notes. Copy the folders as folders instead of putting every note into one location.

    Steps 5–9

    Open the Obsidian vault and paste the folders

    Now navigate through the iPhone's Files app to the location where Obsidian stores local vaults. The path shown in the screenshots is:

    On My iPhone → Obsidian → My Notes
    06

    Open On My iPhone

    In the Files app, open On My iPhone.

    07

    Open the Obsidian folder

    Find and open the Obsidian folder.

    08

    Open your vault

    Open the folder for the vault you created earlier. In this example, the vault is named My Notes.

    09

    Paste the folders

    Paste the folders you copied earlier into the vault. The exported folder structure will now live inside your Obsidian vault.

    Final check

    Open Obsidian and check your notes

    Return to Obsidian and open the vault you created. Your imported folders should now appear in the file explorer.

    • ✓ Your main note folders are visible.
    • ✓ Your nested folders remain inside their parent folders.
    • ✓ Your exported Markdown notes are present.
    • ✓ You can open notes from different folders in Obsidian.
    The complete workflow

    SNote Retrieval → ZIP → iPhone Files → Extract → Copy folders → On My iPhone → Obsidian → Your vault

    Important to know

    Your notes are now regular files in Obsidian

    SNote Retrieval exports your notes as files that can be organized in folders. Obsidian then works with those files as part of the vault you created.

    Samsung Notes-specific formatting

    Samsung Notes has features that are specific to the Samsung Notes application. Exported content may therefore not look identical to the original Samsung Notes version when opened in another application.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I put the ZIP directly into Obsidian?

    No. Extract the ZIP first, then copy the extracted folders into your Obsidian vault.

    Where do I put the extracted folders?

    For a local vault, navigate through Files to On My iPhone → Obsidian → your vault , then paste the folders there.

    Do I need to create every Samsung Notes folder again?

    No. The exported folders can be copied into the vault as folders, so you do not have to recreate the folder hierarchy manually.

    What if I cannot find my Obsidian vault in Files?

    Make sure you created a local vault in Obsidian and then check the Files app under On My iPhone → Obsidian .

    Can I use a different vault name?

    Yes. The name shown in this guide is only an example. Open the vault folder that you actually created.

    Need to export your Samsung Notes first?

    SNote Retrieval can extract your Samsung Notes and preserve the folder organization in the exported files. Once you have the ZIP, come back to this guide to import it into Obsidian on your iPhone.
